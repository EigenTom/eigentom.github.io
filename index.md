## About Me

I am a current Master student (Master of Science in Applied Computing programme) at the University of Toronto. My research interests lies broadly in Multi-Modal Language Model, Reinforcement Learning, Agentic LLMs and Generative AI. 

I am currently working as a full-time co-op applied research intern at ModiFace (L'Oréal's AI Lab) focusing on controllable video generation.
