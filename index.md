## About Me

I am a current Master student (Master of Science in Applied Computing programme) at the University of Toronto. My research interests lies broadly in Multi-Modal Language Model, Reinforcement Learning, Agentic MLLMs and Generative AI. 

I am currently working as a full-time co-op applied research intern at [ModiFace](http://modiface.com) (L'Oréal's AI Lab) focusing on controllable video generation. I am also working as a part-time research intern in [TIGER Lab](https://wenhuchen.github.io/lab.html) under the supervision of Prof. Wenhu Chen, working on a project focusing on Agentic-RL based Tool-calling LLMs. Prior to that, I worked as an research intern at [Opus AI Research](https://www.opus.pro), where I publiched two papers in top conferences.

I am actively seeking research collaborations and research internship opportunities in MLLM Post Training, Multimodality RL and Controllable Image/Video Generation.
