## About Me

I am a recent graduate from the University of Toronto's Master of Science in Applied Computing programme. My research interests lies in LLM/MLLM Post Training, Reinforcement Learning, and Agentic Systems.

Currently I am working as a part-time research intern at Texas A&M University under the supervision of [Prof. Yu Zhang](https://yuzhimanhua.github.io/), with the topic of LLM Agents. During my master's study, I served as a full-time co-op applied research intern at [ModiFace](http://modiface.com) (L'Oréal's AI Lab) focusing on controllable video generation. Prior to that, I worked as a part-time research intern in [TIGER Lab](https://wenhuchen.github.io/lab.html) under the supervision of [Prof. Wenhu Chen](https://wenhuchen.github.io/lab.html) focused on Agentic-RL based Tool-calling LLMs. I was fortunate to work under the supervision of the wonderful [Jiawang Cao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=UJ1OJPwAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Wenbo Zhu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=z_4-QfQAAAAJ&hl=en) during my research internship at [Opus AI Research](https://www.opus.pro), where I worked on Multimodal Reasoning and MLLM Benchmarking, with two papers published in top conferences.

## Research Interests
- **LLM/MLLM Reinforcement Learning**
- **Agentic Post Training**
- **Multimodal Understanding**
